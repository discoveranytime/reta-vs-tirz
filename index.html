The trial numbers

Average weight loss by dose arm in each compound's landmark trial. Retatrutide's top dose produced greater average loss — in 48 weeks versus 72.

Tirzepatide

SURMOUNT-1 · 72 weeks · GLP-1 + GIP

Retatrutide

Phase 2 trial · 48 weeks · GLP-1 + GIP + glucagon

Fair comparison note: these were separate trials with different durations, populations, and designs — not a randomized head-to-head. The pattern is still striking: retatrutide's highest dose beat tirzepatide's highest dose in roughly two-thirds of the time.

How they work

Same foundation, one extra pathway. That third receptor is the whole story.

Tirzepatide — dual agonist

GLP-1GIPGlucagon
  • Appetite drops — eating less does most of the work
  • Fullness rises — smaller meals feel like more
  • Digestion slows — food stays with you longer
  • Blood sugar steadies — better metabolic control

Retatrutide — triple agonist

GLP-1GIPGlucagon
  • Everything tirzepatide does — appetite regulation included
  • Plus glucagon activity — the differentiating pathway
  • Energy expenditure may rise — more calories burned at rest
  • Fat oxidation & thermogenesis — burning fat, not just eating less
3

Why they feel so different

Same goal, opposite experience. One shuts hunger down; the other turns the metabolic dial up.

Appetite shutdown

Tirzepatide

GLP-1 slows gastric emptying hard — one bite can feel like a full meal, and GIP amplifies the anti-hunger effect. Food can become unappealing, even annoying. It works, but it can feel like dieting through fullness and flatness — and hitting protein or calorie targets takes real effort.

Metabolic drive

Retatrutide

The added glucagon activity raises energy burn directly — metabolic rate, fat oxidation, thermogenesis — instead of making eating miserable. People often describe it as smoother: you can still eat, you just don't want as much. Appetite regulated, not annihilated.

Which one fits your goal?

Neither is "better" in the abstract — it depends what you're optimizing for.

Tirzepatide may fit if…

  • Appetite control is your biggest challenge
  • You want an approved medication with extensive clinical data
  • You prefer stronger hunger suppression

Retatrutide may fit if…

  • Maximum weight loss is the priority
  • You want appetite regulation plus the glucagon pathway
  • You prefer a potentially more metabolism-driven approach
Want max appetite shutdown, or struggle with binge eating?

Tirzepatide's stronger hunger suppression is the tool built for that job.

Want the strongest results with better sustainability?

Retatrutide's trial ceiling plus easier eating may mean easier protein intake and less muscle loss.

5

Bottom line

On the clinical results, retatrutide wins — by a lot.

Higher average weight loss at the top dose, achieved in 48 weeks instead of 72. That said, tirzepatide is extremely effective and carries the deeper evidence base plus approved-medicine status. Retatrutide has demonstrated the higher weight-loss ceiling in trials so far — the rest is trade-offs.
